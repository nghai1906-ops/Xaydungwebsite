Today, I would like to introduce my university to you. This is Phenikaa University.

Phenikaa University is the most beautiful university in Hanoi. The campus is very large, so you might find it difficult to get around when you first arrive. Below is the map of Phenikaa University.

  2. Phenikaa University Faculty of Information Technology
  3. Phenikaa University School of Tourism
  4. Phenikaa University Faculty of Economics
  5. Phenikaa University of Interdisciplinary Digital Technology
  6. Phenikaa University of Foreign Languages and Social Sciences
  7. Phenikaa University Faculty of Medicine and Pharmacy
  8. Phenikaa Faculty of Law
  9. Phenikaa Faculty of Basic Sciences
  10. Phenikaa PIAS Institute
